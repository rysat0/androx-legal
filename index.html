Good questions.

Is my data really private?

Yes. Your health data and logs are stored only on your device — never sent to our servers, advertisers, analytics, or AI. The only thing processed off-device is the email used to sign in. See the privacy policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Androx is a general wellness app. It does not diagnose, treat, or measure testosterone or any hormone. Your Hormone Support Score reflects your logged lifestyle habits — not a blood testosterone measurement. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

What does the score actually use?

It's computed on your device from sleep, training, recovery, body composition and daily habits — the lifestyle factors widely discussed in relation to men's hormone health.

Do I need an Apple Watch or any wearable?

No. Androx works with manual logging, and can optionally read sleep, HRV, steps and more from Apple Health if you allow it.

Where is it available?

iOS, for users 18 and older, in the United States and Japan.
